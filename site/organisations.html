Good to know

Keys stay with users

Tesera never holds private keys and never asks for a seed phrase. Your workspace never holds your users' keys either. Each user signs in their own wallet or sets up their own agent key.

Built to carry over

Every network Tesera adds keeps the same tool shapes, keys and limits, so an agent you build for Elysium moves to the next network without a rewrite. HyperCore tools follow once Kinetiq ships its HyperCore hooks.

Elysium fee sharing

Elysium shares a quarter of its sequencer fees with developers of apps that use its block space. That is Kinetiq’s policy for the chain. Tesera is independent of Kinetiq and Hyperliquid.
